Hasköy is a sans-serif typeface family with eight weights, from Thin to ExtraBold, each with a matching italic. It is available as a variable font with a weight axis.

The family supports Latin Vietnamese, Pinyin, and Western, Central and South-Eastern European languages, and includes OpenType features such as stylistic alternates, fractions, tabular figures, superiors and inferiors, and arrows.

To contribute, see github.com/ertekinno/haskoy.
